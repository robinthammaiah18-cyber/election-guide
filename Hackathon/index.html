Democracy Foundation

What Are Elections & Why Do They Matter?

An election is more than a ritual of casting slips; it is the constitutional mechanism by which free people choose their leaders, hold power accountable, and determine public policies.

Popular Sovereignty

All political authority originates with the people. Voting guarantees that government legitimacy is derived directly from the consent of the governed, ensuring equal voice regardless of wealth or background.

Peaceful Accountability

Elections create a predictable, peaceful mechanism to reward capable representatives and replace those who fail their constituents, averting civil instability through institutional trust.

Direct Policy Impact

From school funding and healthcare infrastructure to tax laws and climate policy, election outcomes shape everyday living conditions for millions of households.

Golden Rule on Polling Day: You Have the Right to Ask the Presiding Officer!

If an officer challenges your identity or your name has a typo, do not leave. Request to speak immediately with the Presiding Officer. You can establish identity with alternative government IDs or cast a statutory Tendered Vote.
